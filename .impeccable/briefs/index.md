# Surface brief: index.html (home)

Scope: a single-page site for the physical store. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. Build path: code-led (no comps; the image generators available in this session are paid third-party services).

Audience and job: locals and ETEC students on their phones checking whether Kuro Neko is open, where it moved to, and what it sells. Action: **Como chegar** (directions). Secondary: WhatsApp catalog, ask about stock, Instagram.

Proof on hand: real hours, real address and landmark, real catalog link, three real product photos from the store's own posts. No prices, reviews or counts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the store floor in miniature: a red konbini sign up top, a shelf rail with POP shelf‑talker labels, and the 営業中 / 準備中 plaque that hangs on Japanese shop doors, now telling the truth about the hours. It refuses the dark‑neon grid of equal product cards the category always ships.

OWN-WORLD: Brand red field (the logo disc scaled up to a whole region), label‑white POP tags with heavy condensed uppercase and small Japanese katakana, ink‑black cat as type and the "closed" side of the plaque, bell‑gold only as the lamp on the open plaque. Square-cut labels with 2px corners, a red shelf rail with a darker lip, receipt‑style hours with dotted leaders and tabular numbers.

STORY: The visitor learns in one screen: geek + Asian goods, in Matão's centre, open or closed right now, across from the ETEC. They believe it from the real photos and specifics. They tap Como chegar, or open the catalog first.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile 390×844. Slim header: logo disc + wordmark left, the door plaque right showing live status. Red field: three‑line Anton headline at ~3.4rem, address line with "em frente à ETEC", primary Como chegar (ink on white) and secondary Catálogo. The cat mascot sits on the shelf rail at the bottom edge; the first POP label starts peeking below. Desktop: same order, headline wider, the rail carries all four labels inside the first viewport.

FORM: Konbini shelf with shelf‑talkers and door plaque (option 1 of 4 offered; chosen by the user). seed: none (unavailable)

Signature interaction: the door plaque swings once on its string when the live status lands and flips between 営業中 and 準備中 sides as the hours change (including the lunch break).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
